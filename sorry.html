I'm Sorry, Bhi

A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Hi, bhi. I'm really sorry. Hindi ko talaga intention na matanggal iyon. Alam kong pinaghirapan mong ingatan at alagaan iyon, and I understand kung gaano iyon kahalaga sa'yo. I know na malaking bagay iyon, especially because it helps you build your confidence.

I know na hindi basta-basta mabubura ng simpleng sorry ko yung disappointment at galit na nararamdaman mo sa akin. Pero I sincerely hope na kahit papaano, maibsan nito yung bigat ng nararamdaman mo.

I'm not saying sorry just to make things okay or para matapos na yung galit mo sa akin. I'm saying sorry because I genuinely regret what happened, and I know na nagkamali ako. Hindi talaga ako mapakali knowing na nasaktan at na-disappoint kita, lalo na kapag nakikita kong umiiyak ka dahil sa nangyari.

I understand if you need time and if hindi mo pa ako kayang patawarin ngayon. I won't rush you or force you to accept my apology. I respect whatever you're feeling right now because I know I have no right to demand immediate forgiveness.

I also know na hindi sapat ang mga salita para maitama yung nagawa ko. Kaya I want to show through my actions na sincere ako sa paghingi ng tawad. I'll be more careful and mindful next time because I don't want something like this to happen again.

I know I can't undo what happened, but I hope you'll give me the chance to make things right in time. I never intended to hurt you, and I hope someday, when you're ready, you can forgive me.

Again, I'm genuinely sorry, bhi.

With sincere apologies, 💗
